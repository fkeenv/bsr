# Membership role does not gate Member-side Statement of Account access

A live Membership on a Property — owner or resident — may open that Property's Statement of
Account and declare a Payment. Owner versus resident only matters for Officer and Administrator
eligibility (and display). Restricting the SoA to owners would block the person who often actually
pays; treating the roles as identical on the Member surface matches how money already moves.

One person may hold live Memberships on several Properties. Their dashboard is a Property switcher
with no combined cross-Property total — a combined figure would reintroduce a person-centric
ledger, which ADR-0001 rejected. The printed bill addresses the Property's optional recorded-owner
name when set, otherwise Block+Lot only; it does not require a Membership. On sale, arrears stay on
the Property and resident Memberships are not cascaded away when the seller's owner Membership ends.
