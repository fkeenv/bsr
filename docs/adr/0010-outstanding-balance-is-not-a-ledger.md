# Outstanding Balance is a Property summary, not a running ledger

The Statement of Account stays per Billing Period. Cross-period debt is answered by an
**Outstanding Balance** on the Property — remaining Opening Balance plus remaining Charges after
Prepaid — plus a list of periods marked Paid, Unpaid, or Partial from confirmed allocation only.
Pending declarations never reduce the total. There is no “in good standing” status. Members see
that summary on the Property; Officers scan an unpaid roster (Block+Lot, Outstanding Balance, this
Billing Period unpaid?) and drill in. Generated periods are not folded away. This keeps the
Treasurer’s “what is owed?” question without reintroducing the running ledger the Member view
rejected.

## Considered Options

A person- or Property-centric running ledger of every movement. Rejected: contradicts the
per-month Statement of Account decision and ADR-0001’s Property spine as presented to Members.

Total-only or period-list-only Member views. Rejected: families need both “what do I owe?” and
“which months?”; the total is a derived summary, not a transaction history.

An “in good standing” product status. Rejected: live Membership already gates Member surfaces;
a label with no consequence is glossary noise, and gating invents policy the association has not
asked for.
